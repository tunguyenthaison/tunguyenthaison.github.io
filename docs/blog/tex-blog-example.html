Proposition 2.1 Simple counting for \(p=1\)

Let \(n\geq 2\), \(\omega \in \mathcal{D}(\sigma , C_\omega ,n)\), and \(x\in \mathbb {T}^n\). If \(f\in B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)\) with

\begin{equation*} 1\leq q\leq \infty , \qquad s{\gt}n. \end{equation*}

There exists \(C= C(n, \sigma , s, C_\omega )\) such that

\begin{equation} \label{eq:RateP1Simple} \left|\frac{1}{T}\int _0^T f(x_0+\omega t)\; dt - \int _{\mathbb {T}^n} f(y)\; dy\right| \leq C \Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)} \begin{cases} \begin{aligned} & \left(T^{-1}\log T\right)^{\frac{s-n}{\sigma -n}} & & n {\lt} s {\lt} \sigma , \\ & T^{-1}(\log T)^{2-1/q} & & n {\lt} s = \sigma , \\ & T^{-1}, & & s{\gt}\max \{ \sigma , n\} . \end{aligned}\end{cases} \end{equation}

Proof of Proposition 2.1 ▼

We start with the estimate (assume that \(x_0=0\) and \(\int _{\mathbb {T}^n} f(y)\; dy = 0\))

\begin{equation*} \left|\frac{1}{T}\int _0^T f(x_0+\omega t)\; dt - \int _{\mathbb {T}^n} f(y)\; dy\right| \leq \frac{1}{T}\sum _{j=-1}^\infty \left| \int _0^T \Delta _j f(\omega t)\; dt \right|. \end{equation*}

We will treat \(j=-1,0\) separately. From ?? we obtain

\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} \frac{1}{\pi } \sum _{\xi \in \mathcal{A}_j} |\widehat{\Delta _j f}(\xi )|\cdot \frac{1}{|\xi \cdot \omega |} & \leq \frac{1}{\pi } \Vert \widehat{\Delta _j f}\Vert _{L^\infty (\mathbb {T}^n)} \sum _{\xi \in \mathcal{A}_j} \frac{1}{|\xi \cdot \omega |} \\ & \leq \frac{1}{\pi } \Vert \widehat{\Delta _j f}\Vert _{L^\infty (\mathbb {T}^n)} \frac{(2n+5)(1 + j)}{\delta _j} = C \cdot (1+j) 2^{j\sigma } \Vert \Delta _j f\Vert _{L^1(\mathbb {T}^n)} \end{aligned}\end{equation*}

where \(C = \frac{2n+5}{\pi C_\omega }\left(\frac{16}{3}\right)^\sigma \). Then

\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} \frac{1}{T}\sum _{j\leq N}\left| \int _{0}^T \Delta _j f(\omega t)\; dt\right| & \leq C\cdot \frac{1}{T} \sum _{j\neq N} (1+j) 2^{j\sigma } \Vert \Delta _j f\Vert _{L^1(\mathbb {T}^n)} \\ & \leq C\cdot \frac{1}{T} \sum _{j\leq N} (1+j)2^{j(\sigma -s)} \cdot 2^{js}\Vert \Delta _j f\Vert _{L^1(\mathbb {T}^n)} \leq C \cdot \frac{D_N}{T} \Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)}, \end{aligned}\end{equation*}

thanks to Hölder inequality with \(\frac{1}{q'} + \frac{1}{q} = 1\), where

\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} D_N & = \left(\sum _{j\leq N} (1+j)^{q'} 2^{j(\sigma -s)q'} \right)^{1/q'} & \qquad 1\leq q’ {\lt} \infty , \\ D_N & = \sup _{j\leq N} (1+j)2^{j(\sigma -s)} & \qquad q’=\infty . \end{aligned}\end{equation*}

Case 1. If \(s{\gt}\max \{ \sigma , n\} \) then

\begin{equation*} D_N\leq C(\sigma -s, q’) \qquad \text{for all}\; 1\leq q’\leq \infty . \end{equation*}

Let \(N\to \infty \) we obtain

\begin{equation*} \frac{1}{T}\sum _{j=-1}^\infty \left| \int _0^T \Delta _j f(\omega t)\; dt \right| \leq C(\sigma , n, s-\sigma , q) \Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)}\cdot \frac{1}{T}, \end{equation*}

where \(C = \frac{2n+5}{\pi C_\omega } \left(\frac{16}{3}\right)^\sigma C(\sigma -s, q)\).

Case 2. If \(s=\sigma {\gt} n\), then

\begin{equation*} D_N = \left( \sum _{j\leq N} (1+j)^{q'} \right)^{1/q'} \leq C\cdot N^{1+\frac{1}{q'}} \qquad \text{for all}\; 1\leq q’\leq \infty . \end{equation*}

For the blocks with \(j{\gt}N\), Bernstein’s inequality (Lemma ??) gives \(\Vert \Delta _j f\Vert _{L^\infty (\mathbb {T}^n)} \leq C(n)\cdot 2^{jn}\Vert \Delta _j f\Vert _{L^1(\mathbb {T}^n)}\). Therefore

\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} \frac{1}{T}\sum _{j {\gt} N} \left| \int _0^T \Delta _j f(\omega t)\; dt \right| & \leq \sum _{j {\gt} N}\Vert \Delta _j f\Vert _{L^\infty (\mathbb {T}^n)} \\ & = \sum _{j {\gt} N} 2^{j(n-s)}\cdot 2^{js}\Vert \Delta _j f\Vert _{L^1(\mathbb {T}^n)} \leq C\cdot E_N \Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)}. \end{aligned}\end{equation*}

where

\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} E_N & = \left( \sum _{j{\gt}N} 2^{j(n-s)q'} \right)^{1/q'} & \qquad 1\leq q’{\lt}\infty , \\ E_N & = \sup _{j{\gt}N} 2^{j(n-s)} & \qquad q’=\infty . \end{aligned}\end{equation*}

We deduce that

\begin{equation} \label{eq:EN} E_N \leq C\cdot 2^{-N(s-n)} \qquad \text{for all}\; 1\leq q'\leq \infty . \end{equation}
4

Therefore

\begin{align*} \frac{1}{T}\sum _{j=-1}^\infty \left| \int _0^T \Delta _j f(\omega t)\; dt \right| & \leq C\left(\frac{D_N}{T} + E_N\right) \Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)} \\ & \leq C \left(\frac{1}{T}\cdot N^{1+\frac{1}{q'}} + 2^{-N(s-n)}\right)\Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)} \\ & \leq C\Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)} \cdot T^{-1}(\log T)^{1+1/q'} = C\Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)} \cdot {T^{-1}}(\log T)^{2-1/q} \end{align*}

by choosing \(N \approx \frac{\log _2T}{s-n}\), and \(C = C(n,\sigma )\).

Case 3. If \(n{\lt}s{\lt}\sigma \), then

\begin{equation*} D_N \leq C(N+1)2^{N(\sigma -s)} \qquad \text{for all}\; 1\leq q’\leq \infty , \end{equation*}

where \(C = C(\sigma -s)\). Thanks to 4 we deduce that

\begin{align*} & \frac{1}{T}\sum _{j=-1}^\infty \left| \int _0^T \Delta _j f(\omega t)\; dt \right| \leq C\left(\frac{D_N}{T} + E_N\right) \Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)} \\ & \qquad \qquad \leq C \left(\frac{1}{T}\cdot (N+1)2^{N(\sigma -s)} + 2^{-N(s-n)}\right)\Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)} \leq C\Vert f\Vert _{B^s_{1,q}(\mathbb {T}^n)} \cdot \left(\frac{\log T}{T}\right)^{\frac{s-n}{\sigma -n}} \end{align*}

by choosing \(N \approx \frac{1}{\sigma -n}\log _2\! \left(\frac{T}{\log T}\right)\), and \(C = C(n,\sigma ,s)\).
